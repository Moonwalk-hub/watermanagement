UNDERSTANDING THE CHALLENGE

The Clean Water Problem in Dandora

Access to clean, safe and affordable water remains a challenge for some households in Dandora, Nairobi. Water shortages and unreliable supply make daily activities such as cooking, cleaning and washing difficult.

Some families may spend extra time and money obtaining water from alternative sources. Unsafe water and poor sanitation can also increase the risk of waterborne diseases.

Why this matters

  • Water may not always be available when needed.
  • Some families struggle with the cost of water.
  • Unsafe water can threaten community health.
  • Children and caregivers may spend time fetching water.
